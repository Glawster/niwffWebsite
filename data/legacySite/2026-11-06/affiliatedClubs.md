At Hillsborough Boys FC, walking football is a fully integrated and essential part of the  
club's community. Our walking football section welcomes players of all backgrounds,  
creating an inclusive and supportive environment where everyone can thrive. Being  
part of Hillsborough Boys means that our walking football team shares the same values,  
spirit, and commitment to promoting health, friendship, and a love for the game. We  
believe in fostering a strong sense of belonging, and every player, whether new to the  
game or returning after a break, is a valued member of our team.

By joining us in our walking football community, you’ll not only experience the joy of  
playing the game but also help us continue to spread the word about this growing  
sport. Walking football offers a unique opportunity to remain active while meeting new  
people and building friendships that extend beyond the pitch. We encourage you to  
become an ambassador for walking football, helping to raise awareness so that even  
more people can enjoy this wonderful sport. Together, we can make a difference and  
continue to grow our inclusive and welcoming football family.

ARDS

Scrabo Strollers emerged into the walking football world in 2016, when founding members Craig  
Brotherston and Colin Morrison noticed the growing popularity of the game in England and advertised in  
the local Chronicle newspaper and around various local football clubs for interested players. The first  
sessions were held in the old Ards Leisure Centre (now demolished) in William Street in the town. The  
club attracted enough players to form Over 50s and Over 60s teams, both of which notched up  
numerous successes, winning the National Cup on several occasions and most recently the Over 60s  
NIWFF league in 2024. The club currently plays each week in the new Ards Blair Mayne Leisure Centre  
during the winter and outdoor at Londonderry Park during the summer. The club name is derived from  
Scrabo Tower, built in 1857, a majestic landmark that affords magnificent views of the town and  
Strangford Lough. New players are always welcome so get in touch if you're interested.

BRAVEHEARTS

The H.E.A.R.T. Project Healthy Living Centre is based in west Belfast.

It provides a range of activities to improve and maintain people’s health and well-being including Walking  
Football.

Walking Football was first proposed as an addition to our activity schedule in around 2019. We started off in our

local leisure centre, then moved to Frank Gillen Centre before finding our regular home at Grosvenor Recreation

and Community Centre.

Local character and football enthusiast, Pop Coogan was the driving force behind the team until his sudden

passing in 2023.

The BraveHEARTS walking football team meet to play at 10am every Friday at Grosvenor Rec. which is at 162

Grosvenor Road. We have the comfort of choosing to play indoors or outdoors, depending on the weather.

All our activities have a £2.00 charge.

If you are interested or have a query, give Siobhan Skates at the H.E.A.R.T Project a call on 028 90310346

or siobhan.skates@heartprojectbelfast.com

The BraveHEART’s new kit got its first run out against the lovely lads from CALA in a friendly in memory  
of Owen ‘Pop’ Coogan, who many from other teams would remember. Both teams remembered Pop &  
another falls legend Jim McKee in a minute’s silence prior to kick off. The game was well contested with  
the BraveHEARTs just edging it. Perhaps having Pop’s name on the sleeve gave us that feeling of an extra  
man.

Pop’s family watched from the sidelines and joined us afterwards for a bite to eat and a presentation of  
a framed shirt.

Our sincere thanks to the Coogan family and the lads from CALA for making the journey and sharing in  
the occasion. Big thanks also to Joe, Caolan and Mary from Grosvenor Road for their invaluable help &  
use of their fantastic facilities. 🙏👊⚽️

BANBRIDGE

Banbridge Walking Football Club was formed in June 2024. Currently catering for men aged 50 and over with  
hopefully a women’s club to be added in the future. The rules are simple, the main ones being that players are  
only allowed to walk, and theres no physical contact. These change the nature of the game dramatically. It slows  
the game and develops more precise skills.

The club caters for all ability levels, for those who are simply looking to exercise and keep fit, and others who  
would like to compete in competitions. The social side of the club plays as important a role as the physical side,  
with its emphasis on meeting weekly after training on Saturday afternoons at the Bannville Hotel Banbridge, for a  
drink and to watch the football scores.

The other aspect of the club is to promote and support the work of local charities through fundraising activities.

Schedule

Training sessions are held at Gilford Leisure Centre on Tuesdays from 7-8pm, and on Saturdays from 3-4pm in  
Havelock Park Banbridge.
