Best of luck to our squads as they set off tomorrow for  
Sweden. They will be competing in the IWFF World  
Championship tournament, being held in Malmo.

This year we will contest 3 different age categories, over  
60's, over 65's, and over 70's.

Our travelling squad, along with management, will consist  
of around 40 people. This is a massive achievement for us  
as we are mainly self funded. Anyone wanting information  
on future sponsorship opportunities, please contact the  
NIWFF secretary David Lemon

squeezy.lemon@btinternet.com

On Saturday 7th June, the National Teams will be hosting the  
ROI in a series of friendly matches. The games will be  
played at Avoniel Leisure Centre, and are part of our  
preparation for the IWFF World Championship, being held  
this year in Malmo Sweden.

The Northern Ireland Over 70s team paid a visit to Seaview last week, home of Crusaders and one of the newer walking  
football clubs in NI. It was a very enjoyable experience with three keenly contested games ending with two wins for NI  
and one draw. Afterwards it was time for socialising, sharing conversations and banter with the club kindly laying on  
some welcome refreshments. A big thank you to Crusaders for the invite and hopefully we'll see them again at some of  
our NIWFF tournament days for another few friendly games.

Carryduff Swifts is a new Walking Football Club for over 60s with its first football session planned for Friday 2nd May from 11.00am to 12 noon and every Friday afterwards. The venue is the 3G pitch at Lough Moss Leisure Centre, Carryduff. Anyone interested is very welcome to come along for a game, whether you are new to walking football or you have been playing for a while. Appropriate footwear for a 3G pitch is recommended. Showering and changing facilities are available if needed and wear any kit as bibs will be provided. Contact Eamon Quinn for further details if interested. Mob: 07485 163690, Email:eamonquinn55@icloud.com

Bangor FC Walking Footballers gave a warm welcome to players from the Hampshire city of Winchester at Clandeboye  
Park recently and hosted a mini tournament which was full of excitement, skill and drama. A Classy Winchester A side  
ended up topping the league table with 10pts ahead of Bangor Seasiders on 8pts, Bangor Seagulls 5pts and Bangor Sea  
Lions on 4pts on what was another great day for walking football.

Winchester A claimed overall victory in dramatic fashion after prolific striker Barry Poffley scored a last gasp equaliser in  
the final round robin match against Bangor Seasiders who had led through a Paul Corken strike. The Seasiders were  
within a minute of claiming the title before a defensive mix up spelt disaster. Swift footed Poffley was in dynamic form  
throughout the tournament, scoring six goals including a hat trick in a 5-0 win over Winchester B where midfielder  
Graham Newland added a further two goals. The English side were a real joy to watch but this impressive outfit had a  
close call against a determined Seagulls team who looked to have secured a draw after utility player Richard Worledge  
rifled in a stinging shot from distance, only to see the nemesis of the Bangor teams, Barry spoil the party with another  
late strike.

The Bangor Sea Lions were under siege from kick off, against Winchester A, with last ditch defending, a point blank save  
by goalkeeper Bryan Coults and the woodwork keeping the favourites at bay. However the pressure eventually told and  
Winchester's left sided midfielder Ian Dawson found the net with venomous precision. The Sea Lions did taste success in  
a really polished performance against Winchester B with captain Colin Booth scoring the only goal of the game and then  
drew 0-0 with the Seagulls in another fine defensive display. The Sea Lions then looked to be on their way to an unlikely  
draw with the Seasiders only to gift a goal to Corken.

A resilient Winchester B were really competitive against the much-fancied Seasiders who had to rely on former  
Cliftonville player John Steele to score the only goal of the game but then Winchesters second string had no answer to  
the Seagulls who were now in full flight with midfield playmakers Maurice Mackey and Nigel Barton both scoring a brace  
while centre forward Eddie Clarke made it 5-0 with one of his composed finishes. A much-anticipated clash between the  
Seasiders and Seagulls ended in a 0-0 draw with neither side able to break the deadlock.

At the post tournament function Winchester coach Heather Kirkland was full of praise for their North Down hosts and  
said, “This was our first overseas trip and it has been a very enjoyable experience indeed. It was a very good competitive  
tournament at Clandeboye Park and was played in the right sporting spirit by all the teams.” “The hospitality at Bangor  
FC has been exceptional” added Heather.

NIWFF Referee Training

At the end of November the NIWFF, with great assistance from the IFA, organised a seminar and training event for new referees for  
Walking Football, at Midgley Park. This was a great success and further training games involving teams from Ards, Bangor and  
Hillsborough, were held in Newtownards in January.

A further training match, featuring players from various clubs took place on 10th March, at Lisburn Leisure Centre. We now have a panel  
of referees, William Campton, Alistair Gray, Gerard Brammeld and Ian Getty, who will be overseen by our longstanding senior referee  
Gordon Fletcher.

We really appreciate the referees and hope everyone will welcome them to our tournaments this season, and treat them with the  
respect they deserve.

Thankyou also to all the players who participated in the training games.
