# NIWFF legacy website archive — 6 November 2026

## Purpose

This folder preserves publicly recoverable content from the existing https://niwalkingfootball.com website before redevelopment. It is a reference archive, not approved new-site copy or a production content database. Originals should be rechecked before publication.

## Captured pages

- [home](home.md) — https://niwalkingfootball.com/
- [latestNews](latestNews.md) — https://niwalkingfootball.com/latest-news
- [affiliatedClubs](affiliatedClubs.md) — https://niwalkingfootball.com/affiliated-clubs
- [gallery](gallery.md) — https://niwalkingfootball.com/gallery
- [playerProfiles](playerProfiles.md) — https://niwalkingfootball.com/player-profiles

## Recovery limitations

- Extracted text, headings, links, image URLs and iframe references are preserved where available, but this is **not** a complete website mirror.
- Visual-layout fidelity, CSS, JavaScript, background images, downloadable media and some Canva-export assets may be missing from this first capture.
- Some news, schedules, player profiles and club contact details are historical and must be verified with NIWFF before being reused as current.
- Third-party news articles are linked rather than reproduced.
- No unpublished data, private files, passwords or CMS access has been recovered.

## Follow-up

Recover static assets from public HTML/CSS and identify image ownership/permission, compare page appearance, verify current club listings and events, and use the survey feedback and approved requirements to design the replacement.
